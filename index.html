I am Samuel Karumbi Mwaura, a multi-disciplinary solutions professional based in Nairobi, Kenya. My career was built across disciplines by design — because the most meaningful problems rarely sit within a single domain.

I hold a BSc in Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering from JKUAT and am a Certified Public Secretary (CPS) — a combination that gives me both the technical foundation and the governance literacy to serve complex organisations effectively.

As a NEMA Lead Expert (No. 13143), I conduct Environmental Impact Assessments and Annual Environmental Audits for infrastructure projects across Kenya, ensuring compliance with national environmental legislation while enabling development.

I am also a hands-on trainer. I have reached 350+ youth through field training and bring a three-year engagement on the World Bank-funded National Agricultural Value Chain Development Project (NAVCDP).

I am also a certified AI practitioner (ALX Africa) and Data Protection specialist (Strathmore CIPIT), placing me at the frontier of how technology intersects with professional practice.
